## 2. Nursing Education Trends — June 2026

### AI Adoption in Nursing Education
- **41% of nurses use AI tools frequently** vs. 57% of physicians (Axios survey, May 2026)
- Nurses split on AI: ~50% say it empowers them; only 55% expect AI to save them time within 2–3 years
- Key quote: "There is a clear lack of AI tools designed specifically with nurses in mind" — Amy Hall, nursing school dean
- **Implication for Bio-Sync:** The window for "AI designed for nurses, by nurses" positioning is wide open. Most competitors bolt AI onto existing platforms rather than building nurse-native AI.

### Next Gen NCLEX (NGN) Shift
- Clinical judgment is the new currency — memorization-based pharm content is being devalued
- NGN-style case studies and matrix questions require new content formats
- Content framing around "clinical judgment" + drug classes outperforms generic pharm lists in search
- **Implication:** Rebuild existing pharm content through NGN clinical judgment scenarios. First-mover on this format.

### Student Loan Crisis Impacting Graduate Nursing Education
- 24 states + DC suing Trump administration over new limits on graduate nursing student loans
- Professional degree designation being narrowed — affects NP, CRNA, CNM programs
- Nursing associations (ANA, AANA) actively litigating
- **Implication:** May shift demand toward undergraduate NCLEX prep as graduate paths become costlier. Also creates content opportunity around "affordable NCLEX prep."

### Nursing Workforce Shortage Driving Education Innovation
- Global shortage: 13 million healthcare professionals (WHO)
- U.S. nurse replacement costs: ~$88,000 per clinician
- Health systems building pipelines starting in high school (HCA Midwest Health new nursing campus)
- **Implication:** More students entering nursing = growing TAM for NCLEX prep

### Digital Readiness & Telehealth
- Indian nurses leading digital care transformation (International Nurses Day 2026 theme)
- Nurses now expected to use telehealth, mobile apps, remote monitoring
- "For nurses, by nurses" technology design model gaining traction (Mayo Clinic example)
- **Implication:** Pharmacology education increasingly needs digital/remote delivery context
