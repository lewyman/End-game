- **Suggested blog title:** "Calcium Channel Blockers NCLEX: Amlodipine, Diltiazem & the -Dipine Trap That Fails Students"

- **Description:** Break down the two CCB subclasses every nursing student confuses. Clinical judgment cases: which CCB for which patient, and when to hold.
